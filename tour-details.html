$189,25/Person

Enjoy the Maldives like never before—where luxury meets adventure and romance. Relax in overwater villas, dive into crystal-clear waters, and experience dreamy sunsets, island hopping, and world-class hospitality all in one unforgettable tropical escape.

Highlights

Discover the best of the Maldives with our carefully curated highlights. Stay in luxurious overwater villas, unwind on private beaches, and indulge in rejuvenating spa experiences. Dive into vibrant coral reefs, enjoy snorkeling and scuba adventures, and hop between stunning islands. Savor gourmet dining with breathtaking ocean views, witness magical sunsets, and spot dolphins on serene boat rides. Whether you're on a romantic honeymoon or seeking tropical thrills, the Maldives offers the perfect blend of relaxation, luxury, and adventure.

Basic Information

The Maldives, a tropical paradise in the Indian Ocean, is famous for its overwater villas, clear blue waters, and white-sand beaches. English is widely spoken, and most travelers get a free 30-day visa on arrival. With warm weather year-round, the best time to visit is from November to April. Perfect for honeymoons, luxury getaways, and water adventures.
